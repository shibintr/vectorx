Unlisted Shares

Understanding the opportunity

Unlisted shares represent ownership in companies whose shares are not listed on a recognised stock exchange. Some investors study these companies because they may be at an earlier stage of growth or may have plans that could change their future valuation. Outcomes, however, are uncertain.

What investors may consider

01

Business fundamentals

The company’s operations, financial position, leadership, and growth plans.

02

Valuation

Whether the price being offered is reasonable in light of available information and comparable businesses.

03

Potential exit

Whether a suitable buyer or future liquidity event may be available.

04

Risk and time horizon

Unlisted shares can be difficult to sell, and valuations may be uncertain.

A company’s growth does not guarantee an increase in share value or a profitable exit. Unlisted shares may involve limited liquidity, incomplete information, regulatory or transfer restrictions, and the risk of losing some or all of the amount invested. Any opportunity should be assessed individually using reliable information and independent professional advice where appropriate.

Our role: to encourage informed evaluation and responsible communication — not to imply guaranteed returns or promote an opportunity solely on the basis of past price movements.
