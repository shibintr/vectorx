Our Market Focus

Six areas shaping global financial markets

Forex Stocks Futures & Options Commodities Digital Assets Trading Platforms
01 · Forex

FOREX – Global Currency Markets

The World’s Largest and Most Dynamic Financial Market

The Forex market plays a vital role in the global financial ecosystem, connecting economies, businesses, financial institutions and investors across the world.

Over the past five years, the foreign exchange industry has experienced significant expansion, supported by international trade, global investment flows, digital trading infrastructure and increasing demand for currency risk management. According to the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), global Forex trading volume reached approximately $9.6 trillion per day in April 2025, representing around 28% growth compared with 2022.

$9.6TAverage daily Forex trading volume, April 2025 (BIS)
~28%Growth in trading volume compared with 2022

Looking ahead

Technological innovation, algorithmic trading, emerging-market currencies and increasing global financial connectivity are expected to continue shaping the evolution of the Forex industry.

02 · Stocks

STOCKS – Global Equity Markets

Powering Global Businesses and Economic Growth

Stock markets are a fundamental part of the global economy, providing companies with access to capital and investors with opportunities to participate in business growth.

Over the last five years, global equity markets have continued to evolve through technological innovation, digital investment platforms, increased retail participation and the expansion of emerging markets. In 2025, global stock market capitalisation reached approximately $151.94 trillion, recording an 18.5% increase compared with the previous year.

$151.94TGlobal stock market capitalisation in 2025
18.5%Increase compared with the previous year

Looking ahead

The future of equity markets is being shaped by artificial intelligence, technology-driven businesses, digital accessibility, international investment and the continued development of global capital markets.

03 · Futures & Options

FUTURES & OPTIONS – Advanced Derivatives Markets

Expanding Opportunities Through Financial Derivatives

Futures and Options markets have become essential components of modern financial markets, enabling participants to manage price exposure, hedge risks and participate in movements across various asset classes.

Over recent years, the derivatives industry has experienced considerable expansion, driven by increased institutional participation, market volatility, electronic trading and growing demand for risk-management instruments. Global futures trading volumes increased approximately 71% between 2019 and 2025, according to Coalition Greenwich research.

~71%Increase in global futures trading volumes, 2019–2025 (Coalition Greenwich)

Looking ahead

Automation, advanced analytics, algorithmic strategies and expanding access to derivatives markets are expected to play an important role in the industry’s continued development.

04 · Commodities

COMMODITIES – Global Physical Asset Markets

Connecting Natural Resources with Global Markets

Commodities form the foundation of the global economy, supporting industries, manufacturing, infrastructure, energy production and international trade.

Over the past five years, commodity markets have experienced significant developments across energy, precious metals, industrial metals and agricultural products. Global supply-chain changes, infrastructure development, geopolitical developments and the transition towards cleaner energy have influenced market demand and investment activity.

  • Energy
  • Precious metals
  • Industrial metals
  • Agricultural products

Looking ahead

The coming years present important long-term opportunities through renewable energy, electric vehicles, industrial expansion, infrastructure investment and increasing demand for critical minerals and natural resources.

05 · Digital Assets

DIGITAL ASSETS & BLOCKCHAIN – The Future of Digital Finance

Transforming the Future of Global Financial Technology

Digital Assets and Blockchain technology are reshaping how value is created, transferred, recorded and managed across the global financial ecosystem.

Over the past five years, this sector has witnessed remarkable technological development, with growing adoption of cryptocurrencies, blockchain networks, decentralised finance, stablecoins and tokenisation. Institutional participation and real-world blockchain applications have also expanded. According to TRM Labs, South Asia emerged as the fastest-growing region for crypto adoption in 2025, with India among the leading countries.

  • Cryptocurrencies
  • Blockchain networks
  • Decentralised finance
  • Stablecoins
  • Tokenisation

Looking ahead

Blockchain infrastructure, asset tokenisation, digital payments, smart contracts and the integration of traditional finance with digital technologies are creating new possibilities for the future of global finance.

06 · Trading Platforms

INTERNATIONAL TRADING PLATFORMS

International trading platforms are transforming the way individuals and institutions access financial markets across the world. Through advanced digital infrastructure, investors can explore multiple asset classes, access international market information and participate in global financial ecosystems through integrated trading technologies.

Over the past five years, international online trading platforms have experienced substantial development, driven by increasing internet accessibility, mobile-based investing, cross-border financial connectivity and growing retail investor participation. The global online trading platform market was valued at approximately $10.9 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach around $19.9 billion by 2033, reflecting a growing demand for digital financial services.

$10.9BGlobal online trading platform market value, 2025
$19.9BProjected market value by 2033

Key Growth Drivers

  • Global Market Connectivity
  • Multi-Asset Trading Access
  • Advanced Financial Technology
  • Cross-Border Investment Infrastructure
  • AI-Powered Trading Solutions
  • Digital Financial Innovation

Looking ahead

Artificial intelligence, advanced trading technologies, multi-asset integration, cross-border investment infrastructure and blockchain-powered financial services are expected to create new possibilities for international trading platforms.

As global financial markets become increasingly interconnected, international trading platforms are positioned to play an important role in the next generation of digital finance.

Our Vision

Building a Smarter, More Transparent and Financially Empowered Future

At VECTORX, our vision is to contribute to a more informed, responsible and transparent financial-market ecosystem in India, where individuals have the knowledge, confidence and access to make better financial decisions.
